Metric glossary

One name, one definition: each entry says which variant a number is (raw, opponent-adjusted, weighted and shrunk) and which plays it counts. The same label on the two sites can show different numbers when the variants differ; this page says how. For analysis and education only.

EPA (expected points added)

Team analytics and Forecast all
Change in nflfastR's expected points from before to after a snap, from down, distance, field position and time. 2026 play-by-play is kept on the original nflfastR EP valuation on both sites (nflverse re-published it on a revised scale on 17 Nov 2026; plays already archived keep their original values, later plays are converted with one frozen per-play-type line).

Dropback

Team analytics
A scrimmage play with nflfastR's pass flag: pass attempts, sacks and scrambles. Plays without EPA or a down, kneel-downs and spikes are left out.
Forecast
nflfastR qb_dropback = 1 (includes sacks and scrambles), spikes left out. Nearly the same plays; counts can differ by a few per team.

EPA per dropback

Team analytics raw
Mean EPA over the team's (or allowed by the defence's) 2026 dropbacks. Labelled (raw).
Team analytics opponent-adjusted
League mean plus the team's effect from one least-squares fit on 2026 dropbacks: EPA = intercept + home + offence + defence, team effects summing to zero, no shrinkage, no earlier seasons. Labelled (opp.-adj.).
Team analytics quarterback, opponent-adjusted
A passer's raw EPA per dropback minus the average dropback-defence effect of the defences he faced.
Team analytics expected in a matchup
League mean plus each unit's opponent-adjusted effect, each first shrunk towards zero by how little data supports it (empirical Bayes) and scaled by the 2021-25 calibration for that week. Labelled (opp.-adjusted, shrunk).
Forecast weighted and shrunk
"Team efficiency entering the game" on a forecast game page: as-of values at the forecast cutoff, exponentially weighted towards recent games, prior-season games down-weighted, shrunk towards league average. Expected to differ from both analytics variants.

Success rate

Team analytics raw / opponent-adjusted
Share of plays with EPA above zero; the adjusted version uses the same least-squares fit as EPA.

Overall rating

Team analytics points per game
(adjusted offensive EPA per play - league mean) x plays per game + (league mean - adjusted defensive EPA per play) x plays per game + the kept share of special-teams EPA per game. 2026 plays only, no prior, no shrinkage of the team effects.
Team analytics with 2025 prior
The same, with each unit's 2026 value shrunk towards a prior from its 2025 value (year-to-year slope fitted on 2021-25).
Forecast Team ratings
Opponent-adjusted efficiency in EPA per play, net = offence + defence, re-fitted using only games completed before each moment, leaning on last season early in the year (down-weighted) and shrunk towards league average.

Luck

Team analytics Luck index (points and EPA)
Mean of (wins - Pythagorean expected wins from points) and (wins - EPA-expected wins). +0.75 or more = potentially overperforming, -0.75 or less = potentially underperforming.
Forecast Luck
Wins minus expected wins from net EPA per play in each game (a logistic fitted on earlier seasons). +1 or more / -1 or less get a label. Because the analytics index also averages in the points-based gap, the two can differ by a win or more.

Hit-or-sack rate and pressure rate

Team analytics hit-or-sack rate
Share of dropbacks with a quarterback hit or a sack in the play-by-play (a proxy: free play-by-play has no pressure flag).
Team analytics pressure rate
Pro Football Reference pressures (hurries, hits, sacks) per dropback, from game totals.

Reliability (High / Medium / Low)

Team analytics
How much of a figure is likely to last at its sample size, from the fitted shrinkage weight: High 50%+ kept, Medium 20-50%, Low under 20%.
